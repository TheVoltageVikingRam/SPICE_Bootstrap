    $V_G$ swept: \{0.2,\,0.4,\,0.6,\,0.8\}\,V\\[0.2em]
    \textbf{Extraction:}\; $\displaystyle C = \frac{\mathrm{Im}(I_g)}{2\pi f \cdot V_{ac}}$
    \;\;{\tiny (flat to 4+ sig.\ fig.\ $\Rightarrow$ purely capacitive)}\\[0.3em]
    \textbf{Bias configurations:}\\[0.1em]
    {\tiny
    \begin{tabular}{@{}llll@{}}
        \toprule
        Netlist & Device & D\,/\,S & Type \\
        \midrule
        \texttt{nmos\_cg\_gnd}  & NMOS & D=S=GND & Standard ($V_{ds}\!=\!0$) \\
        \texttt{cgs\_nmos\_vdd} & NMOS & D=GND, S=VDD & Cross-biased \\
        \texttt{cgs\_pmos\_vdd} & PMOS & D=S=VDD & Standard ($V_{ds}\!=\!0$) \\
        \texttt{cgs\_pmos\_gnd} & PMOS & D=VDD, S=GND & Cross-biased \\
        \bottomrule
    \end{tabular}}
}

        \toprule

        0.8\,V & 0.0418 & 0.0388 & 0.0380 & 0.0330 \\
        \bottomrule
    \end{tabular}\\[0.3cm]
    \flushleft
    \scriptsize
    \textbf{Observations:}\\[0.1em]
    $\bullet$ NMOS $C_{gg}$ \textbf{rises} with $V_G$ --- deeper inversion\\[0.1em]
    $\bullet$ PMOS $C_{gg}$ \textbf{falls} with $V_G$ --- $V_{sg} = V_{DD}-V_G$ shrinks\\[0.1em]
    $\bullet$ Std vs.\ cross differ by \textbf{few aF} --- $V_{ds}$-dependent\\
    \hspace{0.35cm} inversion-charge partitioning
}

\noindent
\slidebox{7}{
    \centering
    \textbf{Part B --- Model-Card Estimate}\\[0.2cm]
    \small
    \flushleft
    From the PTM-HP model card:
    \vspace{-0.1cm}
    \begin{align*}
        C_{ox} &= \frac{\varepsilon_{ox}}{t_{ox}}
               = \frac{3.9 \times 8.854 \times 10^{-12}}{1.05 \times 10^{-9}}
               \approx 0.03289\;\text{F/m}^2\\[3pt]
        C_{gg} &= C_{ox} \cdot W \cdot L
    \end{align*}
    \vspace{-0.3cm}
    \centering
    \begin{tabular}{@{}lccc@{}}
        \toprule
        Device & $W$ & $L$ & $C_{gg}$ (fF) \\
        \midrule
        NMOS          & 44\,nm   & 22\,nm & \textbf{0.0318} \\
        PMOS ($k\!=\!1.3$) & 57.2\,nm & 22\,nm & \textbf{0.0414} \\
        \bottomrule
    \end{tabular}\\[0.3cm]
    \flushleft
    \scriptsize
    First-order estimates sit within the range of AC-extracted\\
    values, confirming consistency with $\varepsilon_{ox}/t_{ox}$.
}
\hfill
\slidebox{8}{
    \centering
    \textbf{Part C \& Summary}\\[0.2cm]
    \small
    Inverter input drives both gates in parallel:\\[0.1em]
    \normalsize
    $\displaystyle\boxed{C_{inv} = C_{gg,n} + C_{gg,p} = 0.0318 + 0.0414 = \mathbf{0.0732}\;\text{fF}}$
    \\[0.3cm]
    \scriptsize
    \rule{11cm}{0.4pt}\\[0.2cm]
    \textbf{Consolidated Results}\\[0.15cm]
    \begin{tabular}{@{}ll@{}}
        \toprule
        \textbf{Quantity} & \textbf{Value} \\
        \midrule
        $C_{gg,n}$ (model-card)         & 0.0318\,fF \\
        $C_{gg,p}$ (model-card)         & 0.0414\,fF \\
        $C_{gg,n}$ (AC, $V_G\!=\!0.8$\,V) & 0.0418\,fF \\
        $C_{gg,p}$ (AC, $V_G\!=\!0.2$\,V) & 0.0551\,fF \\
        $C_{inv}$                       & \textbf{0.0732\,fF} \\
        \midrule
        NMOS trend   & $C_{gg} \uparrow$ with $V_G$ \\
        PMOS trend   & $C_{gg} \downarrow$ with $V_G$ \\
        \bottomrule
    \end{tabular}\\[0.2cm]
    \flushleft
    \scriptsize
    \textbf{Takeaway:} $C_{gg}$ is bias-dependent; model-card $C_{ox}WL$\\
    matches simulation. $C_{inv} \approx 73$\,aF sets speed--power trade-off.
}

\slidebox{5}{
    \textbf{Part A --- Method \& Setup}\\[0.2cm]
    \scriptsize
    \textbf{Objective:} Extract intrinsic gate capacitance of NMOS \& PMOS\\
    (22\,nm PTM-HP, $W_n = 44$\,nm, $L = 22$\,nm, $k = 1.3$, $V_{DD} = 0.8$\,V)\\[0.3em]

    \textbf{Simulation:} Spectre \texttt{ac} analysis, $f = 100$\,Hz\,--\,10\,MHz\\
    AC stimulus: 1\,mV;\quad $V_{G}$ swept over \{0.2, 0.4, 0.6, 0.8\}\,V\\[0.3em]

    \textbf{Extraction formula:}
    \vspace{-0.2cm}
    \[
        C = \frac{\mathrm{Im}(I_g)}{2\pi f \cdot V_{ac}}
    \]
    \vspace{-0.2cm}
    Verified \textbf{flat to 4+ sig.\ figures} across full frequency sweep\\
    $\Rightarrow$ purely capacitive, no resonant behaviour.\\[0.3em]

    \textbf{Four bias configurations tested:}\\[0.1em]
    \begin{tabular}{@{}llll@{}}
        \toprule
        \textbf{Netlist} & \textbf{Device} & \textbf{D / S} & \textbf{Type} \\
        \midrule
        \texttt{nmos\_cg\_gnd}   & NMOS & D=S=GND & Standard \\
        \texttt{cgs\_nmos\_vdd}  & NMOS & D=GND, S=VDD & Cross \\
        \texttt{cgs\_pmos\_vdd}  & PMOS & D=S=VDD & Standard \\
        \texttt{cgs\_pmos\_gnd}  & PMOS & D=VDD, S=GND & Cross \\
        \bottomrule
    \end{tabular}
}
\hfill
\slidebox{6}{
    \textbf{Part A --- AC-Extracted $C_{gg}$ Results}\\[0.3cm]
    \centering
    \begin{tabular}{@{}r|cc|cc@{}}
        \toprule
        & \multicolumn{2}{c|}{\textbf{NMOS}} & \multicolumn{2}{c}{\textbf{PMOS}} \\
        $V_G$ (V) & Standard & Cross & Standard & Cross \\
        \midrule
        0.2 & 0.0314 & 0.0276 & 0.0551 & 0.0494 \\
        0.4 & 0.0329 & 0.0334 & 0.0457 & 0.0461 \\
        0.6 & 0.0399 & 0.0358 & 0.0433 & 0.0397 \\
        0.8 & 0.0418 & 0.0388 & 0.0380 & 0.0330 \\
        \bottomrule
    \end{tabular}\\[0.15cm]
    {\scriptsize All values in fF (magnitudes).}\\[0.4cm]

    \flushleft
    \scriptsize
    \textbf{Key Observations:}\\[0.15em]
    $\bullet$ \textbf{NMOS $C_{gg}$ rises} with $V_G$ --- higher $V_{gs}$ drives deeper\\
    \hspace{0.35cm} inversion $\Rightarrow$ more charge under gate.\\[0.2em]
    $\bullet$ \textbf{PMOS $C_{gg}$ falls} with $V_G$ --- effective $V_{sg} = V_{DD} - V_G$\\
    \hspace{0.35cm} decreases, moving device toward cutoff.\\[0.2em]
    $\bullet$ Standard vs.\ cross-biased differ by \textbf{only a few aF} ---\\
    \hspace{0.35cm} reflects $V_{ds}$-dependent inversion-charge partitioning.
}

\slidebox{7}{
    \textbf{Part B --- Model-Card Estimate}\\[0.3cm]
    \normalsize
    From the 22\,nm PTM-HP model card:\\[0.3em]
    \begin{align*}
        t_{ox}  &= \text{TOXE} = 1.05\;\text{nm (NMOS)};\; 1.1\;\text{nm (PMOS)}\\[4pt]
        \varepsilon_{ox} &= \text{EPSROX} \times \varepsilon_0 = 3.9 \times 8.854 \times 10^{-12}\\[4pt]
        C_{ox}  &= \frac{\varepsilon_{ox}}{t_{ox}} \approx 0.03289\;\text{F/m}^2 \;\text{(NMOS)}
    \end{align*}
    \vspace{-0.2cm}
    Parallel-plate gate capacitance $C_{gg} = C_{ox} \cdot W \cdot L$:\\[0.3em]
    \centering
    \begin{tabular}{@{}lccc@{}}
        \toprule
        \textbf{Device} & $W$ & $L$ & $C_{gg}$ (fF) \\
        \midrule
        NMOS          & 44\,nm   & 22\,nm & \textbf{0.0318} \\
        PMOS ($k\!=\!1.3$) & 57.2\,nm & 22\,nm & \textbf{0.0414} \\
        \bottomrule
    \end{tabular}\\[0.4cm]
    \flushleft
    \scriptsize
    First-order estimates sit \textbf{within the range} of the AC-extracted\\
    values in Part A, confirming physical consistency with $\varepsilon_{ox}/t_{ox}$.
}

\slidebox{8}{
    \textbf{Part C --- Inverter Input Capacitance \& Summary}\\[0.3cm]
    \normalsize
    A CMOS inverter input drives both gates in parallel:\\[0.2em]
    \[
        \boxed{C_{inv} = C_{gg,n} + C_{gg,p} = 0.0318 + 0.0414 = \mathbf{0.0732}\;\text{fF}}
    \]

    \vspace{0.3cm}
    \rule{12cm}{0.4pt}\\[0.3cm]

    \textbf{Consolidated Results}\\[0.2cm]
    \centering
    \scriptsize
    \begin{tabular}{@{}lc@{}}
        \toprule
        \textbf{Quantity} & \textbf{Value} \\
        \midrule
        $C_{gg}$ NMOS (model-card) & 0.0318 fF \\
        $C_{gg}$ PMOS (model-card) & 0.0414 fF \\
        $C_{gg}$ NMOS (AC, $V_G\!=\!0.8$\,V, std.) & 0.0418 fF \\
        $C_{gg}$ PMOS (AC, $V_G\!=\!0.2$\,V, std.) & 0.0551 fF \\
        $C_{inv}$ (model-card) & \textbf{0.0732 fF} \\
        \midrule
        NMOS trend & $C_{gg} \uparrow$ with $V_G$ \\
        PMOS trend & $C_{gg} \downarrow$ with $V_G$ \\
        Std.\ vs.\ cross diff. & $\sim$ few aF \\
        \bottomrule
    \end{tabular}\\[0.3cm]
    \flushleft
    \scriptsize
    \textbf{Takeaway:} AC-extracted $C_{gg}$ is bias-dependent and\\
    consistent with $C_{ox} \cdot W \cdot L$. The inverter input sees\\
    $C_{inv} \approx 73.2$\,aF, setting the speed--power trade-off.
}
